Pulse privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Pulse is a health tracking app (profile, nutrition goals and, as versions come out, meals, workouts, weight, sleep and hydration) designed to work entirely on your device.

In short: Pulse collects, transmits and shares no data at all. There is no server, no account, no advertising and no analytics.

1. Data processed

Pulse only stores what you enter:

  • your profile: first name (optional), sex, date of birth, height, weight, activity level, goal and target weight (optional);
  • your nutrition goals and display preferences (theme, language, units);
  • in versions that offer them: meals, workouts, weigh-ins, sleep and hydration.

This data is only used to show your statistics and estimate your needs, directly on the device.

2. No collection, no sharing

  • Data stays in the app's private storage, on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Pulse includes no analytics, advertising, tracking or remote crash reporting SDK.
  • The app requests no permission giving access to your contacts, location, photos or the Internet.
  • The developer has no technical means of accessing your data.

3. Security

  • Android and iOS: the database is encrypted (SQLCipher, AES-256). Its key is randomly generated on first launch, kept in the system's secure storage (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain) and never leaves the device.
  • Web version: browsers offer no equivalent key storage. Data is stored locally in the browser without encryption at rest; it is protected by your operating system and browser session.
  • Automatic cloud backup (Google) and device-to-device transfer are disabled for Pulse's data.

4. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on the device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time:

  • in the app: Profile → Privacy → Erase all my data;
  • or by uninstalling Pulse, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings.

Once erased, it cannot be recovered by anyone, including the developer.

5. Minimum age

Pulse is intended for people aged 13 and over.

6. Your rights

As the developer collects no data, there is no copy on their side to access, correct or delete: you keep full control of your data, on your device.

7. Changes

If a future feature changes these commitments (for example looking up a product by barcode, which would only send the barcode), this policy will be updated before that feature is released, with a new date at the top of the page.
